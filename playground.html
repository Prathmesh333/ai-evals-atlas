03 · Success once or every time?

This simplified probability model assumes independent attempts with the same success probability p. It is not the finite-sample pass@k estimator.

At least one success: 1 − (1 − p)ᵏ. All attempts succeed: pᵏ. Real task difficulty and correlated attempts can invalidate this simple model.

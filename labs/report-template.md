# Evaluation report

## Decision
State the decision this experiment supports. Separate the measured result from your recommendation.

## Dataset
Describe the population, source, license, sample count, sampling method, slices, label guide, and known gaps.
Record the split strategy, duplicate checks, dataset version, and SHA-256 hashes.

## Systems
Record baseline and candidate model revisions, prompts, tools, retrieval configuration, and decoding settings.
Include context and output budgets, timeout policy, retries, hardware, and dependency versions.

## Graders
Define each metric, normalization, missing-answer policy, and aggregation rule.
Describe grader tests, human calibration, and disagreement handling.

## Results
Report case counts, paired differences, uncertainty, slice results, latency, and cost where measured.
Do not invent unavailable measurements. Label synthetic or simulated results.

## Failures
Document at least three representative failures with case identifiers and probable causes.
Separate model failures from environment and grader failures.

## Limitations and release conditions
List unresolved issues, contamination uncertainty, coverage gaps, and the conditions for release or further testing.
